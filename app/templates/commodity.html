About the source and observation dates

Each series keeps the dates, units, and publication frequency of its source. Daily observations may skip weekends or missing reports; monthly series are published with a delay and may be revised. Retrieval is when BenchmarkWatcher last fetched the source, not the date of the price.

Changes compare available observations. The selected chart range ends at the latest available observation. This is historical reference data, not real-time quotes or investment advice.
