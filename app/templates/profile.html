How estimates are calculated

Provider-reported tokens × published USD rates checked October 2, 2026. DeepSeek estimates account for cached input and off-peak rates; a range is shown when the cache split or holiday tariff is uncertain. Jev charges for input tokens only. Credits, tax, custom plans, later price changes and other apps can change your invoice.

Missing usage, unknown models and failed requests stay unpriced. A response can incur a charge even when its answer is rejected. “Response received” describes the API response, not whether an analysis was saved. Estimates are stored with each request and do not reprice earlier activity.
