Support

Help with historical benchmarks, company research, account access and privacy.

Contact us

Email benchmarkwatcher@aulenor.com. Include the page URL and a short description of what happened. For a data question, include the benchmark or company name and the observation or filing date.

Never send your password, recovery code or AI provider key. For an account-data request, tell us your username in the email; we may need to verify that the request comes from the account owner.

Find an answer

The Help guide explains company reports, calculations and optional AI connections. Source observations can arrive late or change; open a benchmark or report’s source link to check the original publication.

For a public bug report or feature idea, use GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so use email for account or privacy matters.

Your data

Read the Privacy page for what stays in your browser, what is saved with an account and when a connected AI provider receives information. Email us to ask about access to or removal of your account data.
